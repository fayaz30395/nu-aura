Agent: product | Date: 2026-06-10 | Scope: P0 modules (employees, leave, attendance, payroll, approvals) + sidebar/route integrity + incompleteness markers across frontend & backend.

**Method:** (1) enumerated all 264 `frontend/app/**/page.tsx` routes and cross-checked every `href` in `frontend/components/layout/menuSections.tsx`; (2) swept frontend+backend for TODO/FIXME/"coming soon"/"not implemented"/`UnsupportedOperationException`/disabled buttons/empty handlers; (3) verified P0 list pages have create/edit/detail counterparts and forms post to real backend controllers (e.g. `leave.service.ts` → `LeaveRequestController` `/api/v1/leave-requests` — match); (4) checked loading/error/empty states on P0 pages.

SuperAdmin bypass and My Space no-permission items confirmed BY DESIGN (documented at `menuSections.tsx:211-214`).
