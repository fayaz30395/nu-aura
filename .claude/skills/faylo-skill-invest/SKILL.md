# faylo-skill-invest

Auto-loads for `faylo-prd-creator` at Stage 03. Every user story in a PRD must be Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, and Testable. A story that fails Estimable or Small is a signal to split it before it reaches Stage 06 ticket generation, not after.
